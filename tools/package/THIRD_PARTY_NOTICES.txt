FFmpeg (avcodec-61.dll, avutil-59.dll, swscale-8.dll, swresample-5.dll) - GNU LGPL version 2.1 or later
  Used unmodified as separate DLLs (dynamic linking) to decode the game's MPEG-2 movies. You may replace these
  DLLs with your own build of the same FFmpeg major versions. Source code and the license text:
  https://ffmpeg.org/ (LGPL 2.1: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html)
